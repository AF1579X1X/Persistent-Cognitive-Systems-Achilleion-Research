A dated public status of the ACHILLEION/Q_ANIMA persistent cognitive and epistemic research architecture, distinguishing conception, formalization, implementation, technical exercise, operational traversal and experimental demonstration.

1. Document status, date, version and scope

This document is a dated public research statement with snapshot date 2026-08-04. It reports the status of the ACHILLEION/Q_ANIMA research architecture under the Protocol V1 Freeze. Protocol V2 has not been declared. AUTHORITY=NONE remains invariant. 2. Executive summary

ACHILLEION is a distributed, non-sovereign architectural subject. Its continuity is located in constitutional invariants, relations, provenance and auditable genealogy, not in any single model, writer, memory component or application domain. A bounded epistemic participation kernel has been implemented that provides observational identity, provenance, fail-closed admission, HELD/suspension, rejection, re-observation, an in-memory revocation transition, receipt generation, atomic transitions, idempotency, input non-falsification and authority separation, and that never auto-promotes an object to truth, belief, memory, certification, decision or authority. These properties are implemented within the bounded, non-persistent kernel perimeter; they are not demonstrated as end-to-end properties of the entire architecture or as a persistent operational path.

6. REALIZATION / SIGNIFICANCE / LIMIT analysis

For every significant public assessment, this site distinguishes REALIZATION (what materially exists and which artifacts support it), SIGNIFICANCE (what capability that realization makes possible) and LIMIT (what the evidence does not authorize us to conclude). Where evidence is insufficient, the status is NOT_DEMONSTRATED.

7. Bounded VC-EP1 implementation status

A bounded epistemic participation kernel exists in the private research perimeter. The following are IMPLEMENTED within the bounded, non-persistent kernel perimeter; not demonstrated as end-to-end properties of the entire architecture or as a persistent operational path: observational identity, provenance, verifiable state, fail-closed admission, HELD/suspension, rejection, re-observation, an in-memory revocation transition, receipt generation, atomic transitions, idempotency, input non-falsification, authority separation and the absence of automatic promotion. Test definitions were inspected; a prior audit reported observed executions; those results were not reproduced during the normalization addendum; and no durable committed execution receipt currently preserves the command, environment, output and source-state binding.

The source implementation and test definitions inspected during the evidence review matched a private provenance reference. The association of the previously reported execution results with that source state was reported by the prior audit but is not preserved through a durable, independently inspectable execution receipt.
