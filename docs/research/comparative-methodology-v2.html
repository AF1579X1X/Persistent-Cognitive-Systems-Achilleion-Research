Comparative Methodology V2

A proposed neutral and dated comparative method, designed for future reproducible application without treating category proximity as superiority.

1. Document status and snapshot date

This dated public research statement has snapshot date 2026-08-04. The Protocol V1 Freeze remains in force; “V2” identifies the second version of this methodology and does not declare Protocol V2. AUTHORITY=NONE remains invariant. The methodology is CONCEIVED; the explicit criteria in this document constitute a proposed formalization. Evidence quality: DOCUMENTATION_ONLY. Publishing this statement does not promote any architectural capability. Controlled baseline comparison remains NOT_DEMONSTRATED.

2. Purpose and research questions

The method asks what each comparison unit is, what its authoritative sources document, which dimensions are applicable, and which conclusions exceed the available evidence. It is intended to support a repeatable source review and a future controlled experiment. It does not turn documentary proximity into equivalence, uniqueness, superiority or demonstrated cognitive advantage.

3. Relation to the V1 comparative article

The existing Italian V1 article is preserved as a dated genealogical snapshot with its own historical evidential frame. V2 adds category separation, claim-to-source binding and explicit uncertainty handling. It neither silently replaces V1 nor declares Protocol V2.

4. Unit of comparison and selection criteria

The unit of comparison is a named, source-resolvable entity at a defined granularity: architecture, research proposal, software project, framework, protocol, product, hardware platform or domain-specific operational system. A family name is split when it covers materially different entities or generations, as with a current platform and a historical project.

An item is included only when it is relevant to at least one declared dimension, has an authoritative primary source accessible at the snapshot, and can be described without relying on search snippets or third-party summaries. Exclusion reasons are recorded: no authoritative source, category duplication, obsolete item without genealogical value, or no applicable comparison dimension. A second researcher should be able to reproduce the decision from these rules.

5. Category separation

The method distinguishes academic cognitive architectures; LLM memory architectures; agent orchestration frameworks; autonomous-agent projects; local persistent operational systems; commercial platforms; open protocols; edge development platforms; hardware platforms; domain-specific systems; reference literature; and ACHILLEION/Q_ANIMA as the research architecture under examination. A protocol is not treated as a product, a hardware platform as an agent, or a research taxonomy as an executable implementation.

6. Evidence hierarchy and conflict resolution

  1. Normative standards and protocol specifications for protocol requirements.
  2. Original peer-reviewed papers or author manuscripts for research proposals and reported experiments.
  3. Official technical documentation for current product and framework behavior.
  4. Official repositories for project identity, genealogy and source-visible features.
  5. First-party announcements only for dated release context, never as proof of observed behavior.

When sources conflict, the record preserves the conflict instead of selecting the stronger claim. A normative specification governs protocol requirements; current official documentation governs documented product behavior; a repository governs its own stated project genealogy; a paper governs only the study it reports. No source type proves real-world behavior beyond its scope.

8. Comparison dimensions

Dimensions are defined before data collection: persistence across invocations or restart; state inspectability; memory representation and access; provenance; correction or revocation; replay or recovery; permission and safety gating; human control; operational scope; maturity disclosed by the source; local or remote data placement; and epistemic governance. Each cell addresses only one comparison unit and the evidence available for that dimension.

9. Evidence-outcome procedure

  • Documented in cited source: the exact source describes the bounded property. This is not an independent observation.
  • IMPLEMENTATION_DEPENDENT: the protocol, framework or platform permits multiple behaviors and does not impose one result.
  • UNKNOWN: the reviewed authoritative sources do not resolve the property.
  • NOT_APPLICABLE: the dimension is outside the nature of the comparison unit; a reason is mandatory.
  • NOT_DEMONSTRATED: a stronger empirical or cognitive conclusion is unsupported by the reviewed evidence.

Unscored qualitative adjectives are prohibited as free ratings. A qualitative term may appear only inside a source title or as an explicitly attributed source claim with a defined rubric.

10. Treatment of vendor and project claims

First-party descriptions are recorded as documented capabilities or claims. Optional features are not treated as defaults; defaults are not treated as structural necessities; a platform capability is not attributed to every application built on it. Public documentation is not evidence that a feature was enabled, exercised or effective in a specific deployment.

11. Absent, inaccessible and changing evidence

An inaccessible source produces UNKNOWN, not a guessed value. When a source changes after the snapshot, the V2 record remains tied to 2026-08-04 and a later edition must record a new snapshot rather than silently rewriting the old one. Version numbers, preview status and release status are included only when an authoritative source states them.

12. REALIZATION / SIGNIFICANCE / LIMIT method

REALIZATION names the source-bound artifact or behavior. SIGNIFICANCE states the capability it makes possible without claiming effectiveness. LIMIT identifies the unsupported inference. Every system note and every ACHILLEION/Q_ANIMA claim uses this three-part structure.

13. Baseline framework

A future experiment will compare: a stateless LLM; the same model with ungoverned memory; a conventional persistent agent; and ACHILLEION with its constitutional and epistemic chain. These are experimental configurations, not labels applied to the documentary map. No controlled baseline experiment has yet been performed.

14. Controls required for a future experiment

The protocol must pre-register tasks, datasets, scoring rules, failure criteria and stopping rules. It must hold constant the model version, decoding settings, prompts, tools, input order, external data, hardware class, context budget and allowed wall time. Total cognitive cost must include model tokens, tool calls, storage operations, retries, human interventions, elapsed compute and failure recovery. When exact equality is impossible, the mismatch and its expected effect must be reported before results are interpreted.

15. Replication requirements

A result is replicated only when a separate operator or team runs the frozen protocol in an independently prepared environment, retains source-state and execution receipts, and reproduces the predefined outcome within the declared tolerance. Re-running the same harness by the same operator is repetition, not independent replication.

16. Cross-domain transfer requirements

Transfer requires a capability defined before testing, demonstrated in the first domain, then evaluated in a second domain with different data and task semantics under the same constitutional controls. Reusing terminology, prompts or infrastructure is not by itself transfer. Cross-domain transfer remains NOT_DEMONSTRATED.

17. RCC as a research formulation

RCC = Certified Cognitive Value / Total Cognitive Cost is a CONCEIVED research formulation, not an empirically validated metric. A future protocol must define the numerator, certification procedure, denominator and uncertainty before calculation. Any observer of value or cost retains AUTHORITY=NONE and may observe, measure, compare and propose, but may not certify, mutate, decide, execute or cross the Safety Boundary.

18. Source, claim and snapshot policy

Each material cell must map to at least one exact source link. One source may support several claims only when each mapping is explicit. The snapshot date is 2026-08-04; access date and source-disclosed temporal qualifiers are recorded. Search-result snippets and unsourced summaries are never evidence records.

19. Limitations and NOT_DEMONSTRATED set

This is a proposed method designed for future reproducible application. Documentary comparison does not establish observed behavior, causal advantage or controlled equivalence. Controlled baseline comparison, independent replication, cross-domain transfer, RCC empirical validation, certified cognitive advantage, uniqueness and superiority remain NOT_DEMONSTRATED.
